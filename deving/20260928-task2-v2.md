# Task 2 v2: extensions, BM25 views and rival features (2026-09-28)

Goal: move Tasks 2 and 3 towards the top three (Task 2: PAI 57.73, PALI 57.50, nchellwig
56.55; Task 3: PALI 49.20, Takoyaki 48.03, nchellwig 47.19), budget $15–20 including
evaluation. Development on dev only, on top of the frozen 0007 signals (all cached); the
adopted configuration is [log 0010](../logs/0010-st2-st3-extensions-views.md).

## What the winning systems suggest

All leading teams fine-tune generative models; the parts that transfer to pure Jev are
retrieval and aggregation. Takoyaki retrieves with BM25 (k = 50) in three variants and
keeps what the variants agree on; nchellwig and NYCU aggregate several samples
(self-consistency, weighted voting); TeamLasse reports boundary errors as the main error
class; PALI specialises per subtask. Fine-tuning itself is out of scope (Jev only).

## Error decomposition on dev (cache only)

- Gold opinions missing from the candidates were mostly **longer** than every overlapping
  candidate (21–25% of gold opinions on rus/tat/ukr, 25% on zho_laptop): leading adverbs,
  negations, clause heads.
- The rest of the loss: gold pairs in the pool but scored below threshold, or suppressed by
  an overlapping boundary variant of themselves.

## Not adopted

- **Joint V/A calibration** of the pair scores (as in Task 1): +0.03 cF1.
- **Per-corpus thresholds**: up to +1.5 on single corpora in-sample, not stable across folds.
- **Pair Choice** ([`archive/jev/choose.py`](archive/jev/choose.py), runner
  [`archive/tools/probe_pair_choice.py`](archive/tools/probe_pair_choice.py)): Jev chooses
  which opinion candidate (or none) pairs with each aspect and vice versa; as reranker
  features 62.25, no gain over the rival features.
- **16 instead of 4 examples per check**: span-check AUC +0.007 to +0.023 on a sample; the
  check cost scales with the examples, not pursued.
- **More training data for the reranker** (learning curve, 25% → 100% of dev): 59.8 → 62.3,
  still rising, but dev is the only annotated split available to it.

## Follow-up: BIO labels voted over retrieved-example views (dev, Chinese corpora)

Diagnosis of the adopted v2 dev predictions: most missed gold pairs are already in the
reranker pool and simply score below the threshold with no overlapping pair kept (share
of all gold pairs: zho_laptop 0.28, jpn_hotel 0.24, zho_restaurant 0.18). Against the kept
correct pairs, their clearest difference is the BIO-marginal opinion score (zho_laptop
median 0.24 vs 0.55); the example-conditioned span check differs less (0.71 vs 0.80).

Tried: the BIO r3 label questions asked again with 4 retrieved train examples in place of
the invented ones, once per BM25 view (bigram, trigram, word;
[`archive/jev/bioviews.py`](archive/jev/bioviews.py)), and the per-token probabilities
averaged over the four label runs (original + three views). Reranker features: span score
from the averaged probabilities and the minimum over views, per role
([`archive/jev/rerank_bioviews.diff`](archive/jev/rerank_bioviews.diff)).

The averaged labels are a little more discriminating (AUC among candidates: opinions
0.738 → 0.756 zho_laptop, 0.830 → 0.854 zho_restaurant; aspects +0.02), but the reranker

run would have cost ≈ $6). Cost: 19.1M input tokens (≈ $0.80).
